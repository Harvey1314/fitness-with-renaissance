General adult guidance. Food and supplements both count toward daily nutrient intake. Check your multivitamin and supplement labels before combining products.

Vitamin D3

Adults 19–70: 600 IU (15 mcg) per day total. Over 70: 800 IU (20 mcg). Check your multivitamin before adding D3.

NIH vitamin D guide

Magnesium glycinate

Adult men: 400–420 mg/day total. Adult women: 310–320 mg/day total. Count elemental magnesium on the label. The adult upper limit from supplements and medications is 350 mg/day unless your clinician advises otherwise.

NIH magnesium guide

Zinc

Adult men: 11 mg/day total. Adult women: 8 mg/day total. The adult upper limit is 40 mg/day from all sources. Check what your multivitamin already contains.
